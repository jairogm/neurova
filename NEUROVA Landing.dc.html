Before you start

A few practical details.

Can I try it before creating an account?

Yes. The interactive demo above uses sample patients and lets you explore the workspace. Use sample documents only; files added in the demo stay in your browser session.

Can I upload an already-signed consent form?

Yes. Open a patient’s Documents tab and upload a PDF, JPG, or PNG up to 10 MB. NEUROVA shows whether a consent form is attached. You review the file for completeness.

How do I create an account?

Choose Get started to open account creation and continue with Google. If you already have an account, choose Sign in.

Where can I read about data handling?

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms, or contact support@neurova.app with questions about your practice.
